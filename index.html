    UPI "Receive Money" Trap High Risk

    QR Code Inverted Payment

    "I am transferring ₹15,000 for your OLX sofa. Scan this QR code and enter UPI PIN to receive money into your bank."
    • ⚠️ Entering UPI PIN is strictly for DEBITING funds, never receiving.
    • ⚠️ QR contains a pre-filled `upi://pay?am=15000` transfer request.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: You NEVER need to scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive funds.
    Clone NetBanking Phishing Phishing

    Bank PAN/KYC Deactivation Clone

    "Your NetBanking access is disabled. Click https://sbi-customer-kyc-reactivate.xyz to verify PAN, ATM PIN & CVV."
    • ⚠️ Fake domain ending in `.xyz` imitating official bank.
    • ⚠️ Demands ATM PIN and CVV on an unauthenticated form.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: Always type your bank's URL directly into the browser address bar.
    Work from Home Task Trap Financial Fraud

    YouTube Like & Crypto Prepaid Task

    "Earn ₹3,000–₹8,000 daily by liking YouTube videos and rating hotels on Google Maps! Join Telegram @VIP_Task_HR."
    • ⚠️ Pays ₹150–₹500 initially to build false trust.
    • ⚠️ Demands heavy 'prepaid deposits' into crypto wallets to unlock earnings.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: Legitimate employers never ask candidates to pay money to receive work tasks.
    Digital Arrest Extortion Extortion

    Fake CBI/Customs Video Interrogation

    "This is CBI Cyber Branch. A parcel with illegal narcotics in your name was seized. You are under 24-hour Digital Arrest."
    • ⚠️ Threatens immediate arrest and forces 24x7 Skype/WhatsApp video call.
    • ⚠️ Demands 'security deposit' transfer into 'RBI Verification Accounts'.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: Indian law has NO concept of 'Digital Arrest'. Police never conduct court hearings via Skype.
    Electricity Bill APK Hijack Malware

    Remote Desktop Screen-Share Hijack

    "Electricity power will be cut at 9 PM. Install Electricity-Update.apk or AnyDesk to pay ₹10 update charge."
    • ⚠️ Requests installation of third-party APK or remote desktop software.
    • ⚠️ Captures live banking credentials and intercepts 2FA OTPs in background.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: Never install unknown APKs or allow AnyDesk/TeamViewer access to strangers.
    Spin & Win Festival Phishing Lure

    Fake Free iPhone & Gift Voucher

    "Amazon Festive Wheel: You won iPhone 15 Pro! Share to 10 WhatsApp groups and pay ₹299 delivery fees."
    • ⚠️ Demands viral forwarding to compromise friends and family.
    • ⚠️ Collects debit card details for a recurring subscription trap.
    🛡️ Safe Rule: If you didn't participate in an official contest, you haven't won anything.

    CASE DOSSIER #CR-8821

    The 72-Hour "Digital Arrest" & Fake Supreme Court Clearance

    Victim: Retired PSU General Manager, Pune | Threat Vector: WhatsApp Video Impersonation
    💸 Financial Loss: ₹48,50,000
    ⏳ Chronological Timeline of Events:

    Day 1 — 10:30 AM: Victim received an IVR call claiming an urgent FedEx parcel addressed to Taiwan contained 5 passports and 140g MDMA.

    Day 1 — 11:15 AM: Call was transferred to a scammer on Skype posing as 'DCP Crime Branch Mumbai' complete with fake police station background & uniform.

    Day 2 — 02:00 PM: Scammers sent fake forged Supreme Court 'National Security Arrest Warrants' and ordered continuous video surveillance ("Digital Arrest").

    Day 3 — 11:00 AM: Convinced that his bank savings needed "RBI Anti-Money Laundering Verification", victim transferred ₹48.5 Lakhs into designated mule accounts.

    🚩 Psychological Exploitation & Trap:

    Exploited intense panic, fear of judicial defamation, isolation from family, and fabricated legal letterheads with genuine-looking stamps.

    🛡️ The Rakshak Prevention Takeaway:

    Government agencies and police never perform online arrest or demand money into secret accounts. Immediately disconnect and call 1930.

    CASE DOSSIER #CR-3104

    The OLX Furniture Selling "Scan QR to Receive Advance" Scam

    Victim: College Professor, Hyderabad | Threat Vector: UPI Intent / QR Code
    💸 Financial Loss: ₹80,000
    ⏳ Chronological Timeline of Events:

    Phase 1: Victim posted old living room furniture for ₹15,000 on an online marketplace. Within 15 minutes, a buyer agreed without negotiation.

    Phase 2: Buyer claimed to use an Indian Army UPI merchant wallet that required the seller to scan a QR code to verify deposit credentials.

    Phase 3: Buyer sent a QR code of ₹15,000. When scanned, victim was prompted to enter UPI PIN. ₹15,000 was deducted instantly.

    Phase 4: Scammer apologized, claiming a system inversion, and sent a ₹30,000 refund QR code. Victim scanned again, losing another ₹30,000 before realizing the fraud.

    🚩 Psychological Exploitation & Trap:

    Manipulated the myth that scanning a QR code can receive funds into a bank account, reinforced by urgency and fake military identity.

    🛡️ The Rakshak Prevention Takeaway:

    Money sent to you is deposited directly into your bank using your UPI ID or phone number. Receiving money NEVER requires scanning QR or entering PIN.

    CASE DOSSIER #CR-5029

    The Midnight Electricity Power Disconnection APK Hijack

    Victim: Small Business Owner, Bengaluru | Threat Vector: Malicious APK / AnyDesk
    💸 Financial Loss: ₹3,40,000
    ⏳ Chronological Timeline of Events:

    07:30 PM: Received SMS: "Electricity bill unpaid. Power disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM. Call helpline 9876543210."

    08:00 PM: Victim panicked as business was operating. Called the number. The caller instructed him to download an 'Electricity QuickBill.apk' to pay ₹12 update fee.

    08:15 PM: APK asked for Accessibility and SMS reading permissions. During payment of ₹12, malware captured NetBanking login & OTP.

    08:30 PM: Screen went black for 5 minutes. During this period, scammers added a beneficiary and drained ₹3.4 Lakhs.

    🚩 Psychological Exploitation & Trap:

    Time-based deadline panic combined with requesting a trivial ₹12 fee lowered victim skepticism regarding installing an untrusted APK.

    🛡️ The Rakshak Prevention Takeaway:

    Always verify utility bills on official electricity board portals. Never install APK files or grant SMS/Accessibility permissions.

    DEFENSE-IN-DEPTH PROTOCOLS

    🛡️ Safe Browsing Guidance & Safety Precautions

    Comprehensive, actionable cyber security precautions curated by National Cyber Crime Investigation Cells

    🌐

    1. Web Browsing & URL Inspection Precautions

    🔍

    Inspect Domain Anatomy

    Check the domain name spelling meticulously. Scammers use lookalike domains such as sbi-bank-kyc.xyz or paypa1.com to simulate trusted institutions.

    🔒

    HTTPS Does Not Mean Safe

    The padlock icon (HTTPS) only means traffic is encrypted — it does NOT mean the host is legitimate. Over 80% of active phishing portals now use free SSL certificates.

    🚫

    Disable Password Autofill on Unverified Sites

    Never let browsers auto-fill sensitive banking credentials or credit card numbers on links clicked from SMS, WhatsApp, or unsolicited emails.

    💳

    2. UPI, QR Code & Digital Payment Precautions

    📱

    The Golden Rule of UPI PIN

    You NEVER enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money. Entering your PIN ALWAYS results in funds being debited from your account.

    🏷️

    Verify Beneficiary Name

    Always inspect the beneficiary merchant/payee name shown on your UPI app confirmation screen before authenticating any transaction.

    🔍

    Inspect Physical QR Stickers

    When paying at physical retail counters, ensure that the merchant QR code sticker has not been tampered with or covered by a fraudulent sticker.

    📱

    3. Mobile Device & Application Precautions

    ⛔

    Strict Ban on Third-Party APKs

    Never download or sideload .apk files received through WhatsApp or Telegram. Install applications exclusively from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store.

    🖥️

    Never Install Remote Desktop Tools

    Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport on the instructions of unknown callers claiming to assist with KYC, bank refunds, or SIM card updates.

    🛡️

    Review App Permissions

    Audit app permissions regularly. No calculator or torch app requires SMS, Contact, or Accessibility permissions.

    🔐

    4. Authentication, OTP & Identity Precautions

    🤐

    Treat OTP as Hard Cash

    Never disclose One-Time Passwords (OTPs) over phone calls or messages. Bank officials, police, and telecom representatives are strictly prohibited from asking for OTPs.

    🔑

    Enable App-Based 2FA

    Use Authenticator apps (Google Authenticator / Microsoft Authenticator) instead of SMS-based OTPs wherever supported to prevent SIM-swap attacks.

    📡

    SIM Swap Vigilance

    If your mobile phone suddenly displays "No Service" or loses cellular reception unexpectedly, contact your telecom operator immediately from an alternate line.

    🚨

    5. The Critical 2-Hour "Golden Hour" Response Protocol

    📞

    Immediate 1930 Emergency Dial

    If defrauded, dial 1930 within 2 hours. This alerts the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRMS) to freeze illicit funds in transit.

    🏦

    Hotlist Cards & Lock NetBanking

    Immediately notify your bank's 24x7 fraud desk to hotlist debit/credit cards, freeze UPI accounts, and temporarily disable internet banking access.

    📁

    Preserve Digital Evidence

    Capture screenshots of transaction receipts (UTR numbers), chat logs, caller phone numbers, and phishing URLs before submitting your complaint.

    📝 File Official Cyber Fraud Complaint

    All complaints are logged in the Central Complaint Box. If a suspect is identified in multiple reports, the case is automatically escalated to Cyber Security Headquarters.
